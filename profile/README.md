## What We Do

Prem Research is the open research arm of <a href="https://github.com/premAI-io">Prem AI</a>. We build and release tools, models, benchmarks, and infrastructure that let you train, fine‑tune, and deploy AI on your own terms.

Every project is designed to work in **self‑sovereign environments** — your models, your data, your hardware. No vendor lock-in. Just open, auditable code.

---

## Philosophy

We believe that research in AI — especially research that touches sensitive data — must be **private, verifiable, and sovereign**. Every line of code we write assumes the enterprise threat model: untrusted cloud operators, regulated workloads, audit‑grade compliance. We open‑source everything to prove there are no backdoors.
